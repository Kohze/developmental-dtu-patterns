# Source-rights confirmation

On 24 August 2026, Robin Gounder, an author and the repository maintainer,
explicitly confirmed that the authors hold the rights and authority necessary
to publish the author-created manuscript, analysis code, figures, tables, and
derived analysis outputs selected by this component's fail-closed release
allow-list.

This confirmation authorises public distribution under the repository's mixed
licence: MIT for code and CC BY 4.0 for research content and author-created
derived data.

It does not claim ownership of, or grant rights in, third-party raw inputs.
Raw reads, external reference datasets, archived R objects containing source
material, and local working files remain excluded. Public accessions,
provenance records, checksums, and author-created derived results may be
distributed.
